Pyvene × nnsight Integration

Remote, batched interventions for interpretability · in collaboration with Stanford NLP · code ↗

Overview

pyvene is Stanford NLP's open-source library for intervening on the internal states of PyTorch models — the operation underlying activation steering, model editing, and much of mechanistic interpretability. Until now its interventions ran locally, so the model you could study was bounded by what fit on your own GPU.

What I built

I integrated pyvene with nnsight and the National Deep Inference Fabric (NDIF), giving pyvene a remote execution backend: interventions are dispatched to models hosted on NDIF's HPC GPU cluster instead of loaded locally. I also added batched execution so many intervention configurations run in a single pass rather than one at a time.

Why it matters

Together these remove the two limits that kept intervention experiments small. Researchers can now run interventions against most open-weight models — including ones far too large for a local machine — and sweep many interventions at once, making large-scale steering and interpretability studies practical that previously were not. The integration is being upstreamed in collaboration with Stanford NLP.
