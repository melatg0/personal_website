Selected work

  • Self-CTRL Policy-gradient training that aligns what LMs say with what they do, lifting explanation–behavior consistency from 43% to 96% and cutting HarmBench failures 30× (15.0% → 0.5%). Accepted to COLM. read more paper ↗
  • Scheduled Quantization-Aware Training Treating quantization noise as a regularizer for full-precision models. On ResNet-32/CIFAR-10, a stochastic geometric (4→32) schedule reaches 84.8% validation accuracy with a smaller generalization gap than full-precision baselines. Final project for 6.7960 at MIT. read more
  • Evaluating Memory Robustness in Agent Safety Benchmarks Treating an agent's prior history and its rendering as experimental variables in safety evals. On the Inspect Evals agentic-misalignment benchmark, a repeatable Opus 4.1 blackmail effect (44%→62%) is an artifact of 1 of 8 benign histories, and adding rollouts drove agreement with the full panel from 27% to 0%. Extends a 1st-place MIT AI Alignment Hackathon project. read more paper ↗ code ↗
