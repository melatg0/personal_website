  • 2026 · Research Self-CTRL Policy-gradient training that aligns what LMs say with what they do, lifting explanation–behavior consistency from 43% to 96% and cutting HarmBench failures 30× (15.0% → 0.5%). Accepted to COLM. read more paper ↗
  • 2026 · Research Evaluating Memory Robustness in Agent Safety Benchmarks Treating an agent's prior history and its rendering as experimental variables in safety evals, not fixed constants. On the Inspect Evals agentic-misalignment benchmark, a repeatable Opus 4.1 blackmail effect (44%→62%) is an artifact of 1 of 8 benign histories, and adding rollouts drove agreement with the full panel from 27% to 0%; rendering the same session as a summary shifts the rate 15 points. Argues evals should sample plausible benign deployment histories and their renderings. Extends a 1st-place MIT AI Alignment Hackathon project. read more paper ↗ code ↗
  • 2026 · Research Detecting Secret Loyalties SFT'd a Qwen2.5-7B model with a concealed loyalty to a fictional company, then built a generate–filter–retrain distillation pipeline to show that distillation degrades concealment. Across 48 measurements the loyalty survives but concealment collapses (92% → 71–89%) while a content-matched control holds flat (91–96%), with no overlap. Two negative results: an activation probe that detects training-run identity rather than loyalty, and a loyalty-score rise that is a selection artifact. Apart × Formation Research sprint. read more paper ↗ code ↗
